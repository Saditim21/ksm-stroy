# M3 results

What M3 (Многофамилна, its explorer and its block pages) built, and how it measures against spec §10 and §12 (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-ksmstroy-m3m4-design.md`). Measured on 2026-11-17 on `redesign-v2`, site code as of Task 7's commit, on top of `fee6f12`. M2 ended at `01eac18`; M3's plan commit is `8307261`.

## What was built

- **Многофамилна's page, `/projects/mnogofamilna-sgrada`** (Tasks 4 and 5), a v2 route in place of `MnogofamilnaBlockSelection.jsx`:
  - `#explorer`, the opening. The H1 „Многофамилна | жилищна *сграда*“ reads across the seam, then the address, the facts „144 апартамента в 2 блока“ | „16 свободни по данни към 01.12.2025“, and „Запитване“ and „По критерии“.
  - Then the night facade: the lights-off twin, with the lit render showing through every floor that has a free apartment (А-2, А-9, Б-2–5, Б-7–9). The lit floors switch on once, bottom to top: the page's one signature moment, in CSS.
  - The facade works as Golden Residence's does: hover and focus, the dashed lens, the edge panels, the phone switch Блок А / Блок Б, and a first tap that previews in the sheet while the second opens.
  - `#blokove` holds two 8 × 9 block grids. `#spisak` lists all 144 apartments; the list loads as the section nears, and `?view=list` lands on it. `#sgradata` shows the street perspective under a night sky, with the building's facts. `#galeriya` holds three spreads: the two blocks, the street, and the two drawings. `#kontakti` is Многофамилна's inquiry, with Многофамилна first.
  - The dialogs are the plan stage and the unit panel, with the date and „тавански етаж“ on floor 10. The unit panel offers the apartment's own plan on the 18 apartments production maps to one: 16 drawings, since Б-202 and Б-206 share Б-102's and Б-106's.
- **The block pages, `/block-a` and `/block-b`** (Task 6): one template, with Б mirrored.
  - Each is a ledger of floors 10 to 2 beside the block's pinned facade, and a row and its floor light together.
  - On a desktop, the row at the screen's middle follows the scroll.
  - A bare `?floor=7` opens floor 7.
  - `MnogofamilnaSingleBlock.jsx` is gone.
- **One explorer for both buildings** (Task 2): `src/explorer/projects/gr.js` and `projects/mn.js`. Picks carry their project, and Многофамилна has its own inquiry options and copy.
- **The data** (Task 1): a snapshot dated 01.12.2025 (16 free, 6 reserved, 122 sold, 0 blocked), with the facts derived from it. The pages prerender its real numbers with the date.
- **The lights-off twin** (Task 3): `scripts/images/mn-lights-off.mjs` makes it from the lit render's own source, so the two pictures are pixel-aligned.
- **Home's Многофамилна half** now opens the page („Изберете апартамент“), its dated count kept (Task 5).
- **Leftovers taken in:** FR7 and F22 (Task 2), FR8 and FR9 (Task 7, below).
- **Measuring:** `npm run perf` serves the build over HTTP/2 itself, with Lighthouse pinned at 13.6.0 (Task 0).

### FR8 and FR9 (Task 7)

- **FR8.** With a reader's text spacing (WCAG 1.4.12) on a phone of 360 × 640 or smaller, the stage's bar takes a row more and its hint grows. The plan's 250 px floor then left the side column (the counts, the key, the table) no height.
  - The fix: there, and only with that spacing, the plan gives way. It takes at most two fifths of the body, and the side column takes the rest (`src/explorer/explorer.css`, under `@supports (height: 1lh)`). The rule reads the spacing from the body's own line height.
  - It was measured in Chrome on Многофамилна's А-9 and Golden Residence's А-3, at thirteen sizes from 320 × 480 to 1440 × 900. Without the spacing, every box is the same with the rule and without it (26 of 26). With the spacing, only the six sizes inside 360 × 640 change.
  - The side column with the spacing, against without it: 146 px against 127 at 320 × 568 (1 px before the fix); 217 against 168 at 360 × 640 (79 before); 94 against 39 at 320 × 480 (1 before).
  - The reader's spacing also makes the hint taller. So at 320 × 568 the scroll box inside the column is 42 px, against 63 px without the spacing; the counts, the key and the table scroll in it.
- **FR9.** After a failed load of the dialogs' chunk, opening the same floor again now loads it again. Each floor opened is a new opening, which the load's effect follows (`src/explorer/ExplorerContext.jsx`). It costs no first-load bytes: Golden Residence's page went from 162,522 to 162,516 B.

**Многофамилна misses its LCP target:** 3.23 and 3.05 s against 2.5 s. M3 reports this and finishes (spec §10).
- In the unthrottled trace both pages paint the twin early, at 408 and 459 ms, with FCP and LCP together.
- Lighthouse simulates an image LCP on every request that started before the paint, as it does on `/`. That is 466 KiB on the main page:
  - scripts, 206 KiB, of which the motion engine is 51.2 KiB, requested after the load event;
  - fonts, 79 KiB;
  - images, 136 KiB;
  - CSS, 26 KiB;
  - the document, 12 KiB;
  - the availability API's preload and the web manifest, 6 KiB.
- Among the images is the lit render, `mn-dusk-1400.avif`, 66.9 KiB at Medium priority. The lights only switch on 0.95 s after the arrival.

**What M4's speed round must do for Многофамилна**, in spec §10's order, then measure again:
1. Load the motion engine after the LCP paint, not at `load` (Home's step 1). That takes 51.2 KiB out of the window.
2. Give the lit render `fetchpriority="low"` through the `ResponsivePicture` option that Home's step 3 adds. The picture stays in the HTML (without JavaScript or with reduced motion the floors are simply lit), but the first paint doesn't need it.
3. Subset the fonts (`--subset-from`) and defer the non-critical CSS.
4. Give phones a `sizes` that keeps a DPR-3 phone at the 1400 px twin and lit render. It costs no bytes (Task 4's deferred minor), and it keeps real phones within the 60 kB budget.
5. If the pages still miss, the remaining lever is a design change, such as the lights layer later or not at all on phones. That goes to the user with the figures (spec §10).

**Golden Residence against Task 0:** 0.93 s → 0.96 s; its block-a stays at 0.94 s.
- The page's three runs (0.95–0.96 s, and 0.95–0.99 s in a second set) sit just above Task 0's (0.92–0.94 s). The shift is small, but it is not noise.
- Its LCP is text. Lighthouse 13 simulates a text LCP on the requests it counts as render-blocking, and every High-priority script counts.
- M3's new chunks added four such requests to the page's head and took one away: `gr.js` (Task 2's project), the token palette `palette.js`, and the gallery's `Spreads.js` and `Spreads.css` (Task 5) came, and `blocks.js` went. That makes 30 such requests against 27, and 1 KiB more. Its block-a gained one, with fewer bytes, and did not move.
- Its first-load JS is 166 B lighter than Task 0's, and its CSS bytes are the same (24.3 KiB).
- In a second set of runs, `Spreads.css` made non-blocking left the median at 0.96 s, so that stylesheet alone is not the cause.
- Fewer, larger chunks in the page's head should win it back. That is M4's chunk arrangement (ruling T6d); spec §10 leaves shared-chunk changes to M4.

**Home** is at 2.75 s against Task 0's 2.73 s, with 849.4 KiB before the LCP against 850.1. This is the miss M4's Home round addresses (spec §10).

## Deviations from the concept that M3 kept

Each one follows the spec, a ruling or the system.
- **Type.** The page's H1 is at `--fs-slogan`: 82 px at 1440, against the concept's 78 (spec §4.1).
- **Landings.** A deep link's dialogs keep the 36 px EU row above them (ruling T7a, spec §8).
- **The phone sheet.**
  - It has no „8 апартамента“ line, because it is shared with Golden Residence, whose output must not change.
  - It stays open while the page scrolls (M2's ruling; spec §13, decision 4). The concept closes it.
- **System sizes.**
  - The block switch is the system's `.seg`, 42 and 40 px tall, not the concept's 36 px.
  - The rails' counts keep the system's 24 px minimum (Diptych §7.2), not 22 px.
  - On a phone, the stage's close keeps its 44 px target, where the concept's × is 16 px wide. So Многофамилна's meta line, which carries the date, takes two lines.
- **The own-plan plate** is the ivory token, not the concept's #F7F4EE.
- **The focus ring.** A rail under an open edge panel keeps the system's focus ring, and the dashed lens is the visible cue (ruling T6a). The ring's edge shows beside the panel. The concept had `outline: none`, which the spec forbids porting.
- **Copy.**
  - The address goes through `bindAddress`, which binds only „кв. 33“; the concept bound four places.
  - The building's H2 breaks „С магазини / и подземни гаражи“, because the one-letter „и“ is bound to the next word.
  - The drawings' plates carry their production titles (ruling PF6). The spread's live caption holds both titles for screen readers only (ruling T5a).
- **The photos' night skies** are CSS masks cut along each photo's skyline, under a gentle grade, not the concept's baked derivatives. They read the same.
- **The wipe on phones** (ruling T5c). On a phone the building's picture opens from its top, and a wipe with no side opens from the top. The concept's `data-wipe` keeps its side at every width and opens a sideless wipe from the bottom.
- **The block pages.**
  - On touch, the first tap on a floor previews it and the second opens it, as on the main page; the concept opened on the first tap. Diptych binds the two taps.
  - The H1 rises as one masked block; the concept rises per line, 0.1 s apart (Task 6's deferred minor).
  - The floors' section is `#explorer`, not the concept's `#etazhi`, because the explorer's landing, its focus fallback and the smoke's check look for `#explorer`.
  - The phone H1 is `clamp(34px, 10.6vw, 52px)`. At Golden Residence's head size, its long word broke mid-word at 320 px with text spacing.
- **The twin** keeps the limits named above.
