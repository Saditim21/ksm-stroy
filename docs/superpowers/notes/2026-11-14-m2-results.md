# M2 results

What M2 (the Diptych re-skin, Home, the Golden Residence page and its explorer) measures against spec §12, which amends v2 spec §10. Measured on 2026-11-15 on `redesign-v2`, site code as of `4e47556`. These are the first Diptych figures; spec §12 asks for them to re-base the "bytes before LCP" budget.

## How it was measured

- **Builds:**
  - performance, accessibility and Best Practices: `npm run build`, a preview-stage build (robots `Disallow: /`, `noindex`);
  - SEO: `VITE_SITE_STAGE=production npm run build`, built locally, served once for Lighthouse and rebuilt normally straight after. It was never deployed, and no inquiry was sent from it.
- **Server:** `node scripts/smoke/static-server.mjs` on `http://127.0.0.1:4190`, as for the M1 baseline. It applies `vercel.json`, compresses text over 1 KiB (brotli quality 5) and runs `api/availability.js` over the saved sheets. It serves HTTP/1.1, where Vercel's edge serves HTTP/2 and HTTP/3.
- **Lighthouse:** Lighthouse 13.6.0 through npx with its default mobile settings: a 412×823 screen at DPR 1.75, simulated slow 4G (150 ms RTT, 1.6 Mbit/s) and a 4× CPU slowdown. Each URL ran 3 times, and the tables show medians. The two commands:
  - `npm run perf -- --categories=performance,accessibility,best-practices,seo http://127.0.0.1:4190/ http://127.0.0.1:4190/projects/golden-residence http://127.0.0.1:4190/projects/golden-residence/block-a`;
  - the same with `--categories=seo` on the production-stage build.
- **"Bytes before LCP"** is measured as the M1 baseline defines it: the transfer size of every request the page started before the LCP was painted, in each run's own unthrottled trace.
- **Machine:** Windows 11, Node 22.23.4, Chrome 157.0.8103.78.

## The miss: LCP on `/`, 4.63 s against 2.5 s

It is not fixed in M2. The cause is not local: neither an image width nor a preload moves it.

**Where the time goes.**
- In the unthrottled trace the hero picture loads in 78 ms (from 53 to 131 ms after the navigation) and paints at 401 ms, yet Lighthouse simulates its LCP at 4.6 s. The simulation counts every request that started before the observed paint.
- On `/` that is 864 KiB. 528 KiB of it is images below the fold:
  - the three services photos;
  - the six works covers, stacked at one place on the phone.
- Lighthouse's Chrome fetches them within the first 125 ms, `loading="lazy"` notwithstanding. They are exactly the pictures within 2,500 px of the screen's foot: Chrome's lazy-load distance where it reads the connection as unknown or slow. Where it reads 4G, the distance is 1,250 px: this machine's own headless Chrome reports 4G and fetches none of them.
- The scripts (210 KiB) and the fonts (80 KiB) share the same simulated 1.6 Mbit/s.

**What was tried**, each on a copy of the build, with 3 runs:

| Change to `/` | Observed paint (FCP, LCP) | Simulated LCP |
|---|---|---|
| none | 173, 401 ms | 4.63 s |
| the hero `<img>` without `decoding="async"` | 151, 315 ms | 4.61 s |
| with `decoding="sync"` | 143, 306 ms | 4.61 s |
| a `<link rel="preload" as="image" fetchpriority="high">` for the hero in the head | 279, 279 ms | 4.69 s |
| no intro (the gate's `intro` class removed) | 220, 262 ms | 4.61 s |
| no services or works pictures (465 KiB before LCP) | 262, 262 ms | 4.02 s |

The preload brings the observed LCP onto the first paint and still leaves the simulation at 4.7 s.

**Two diagnostics, not budget figures.** These were single runs, each recorded with the method used.
- **HTTP/2.** The same build behind HTTP/2, as Vercel's edge serves it (a local TLS wrapper around the static server), simulates `/` at 2.74 s and the Golden Residence page at 0.93 s.
  - On the HTTP/1.1 server, Chrome opens 6 connections, and about 30 first-load requests queue for them.
  - The hero picture's request starts at 634 ms and ends at 4,354 ms under applied throttling.
- **Applied throttling** (`--throttling-method=devtools`, on `/` and the Golden Residence page). This throttles the browser instead of simulating it, and puts both pages over 2.5 s:

  | URL | Server | FCP | LCP | TBT |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | `/` | HTTP/1.1 | 2.24 s | 4.40 s | 65 ms |
  | `/` | HTTP/2 | 3.09 s | 3.11 s | 57 ms |
  | `/projects/golden-residence` | HTTP/1.1 | 2.31 s | 4.61 s | 223 ms |
  | `/projects/golden-residence` | HTTP/2 | 3.02 s | 3.66 s | 257 ms |

  At 1.6 Mbit/s, the rebuilt pages' first loads take about 1.4 s to transfer before any picture: about 160 KiB of JS, 80 KiB of fonts and 25 KiB of CSS. The Golden Residence page's simulated 0.92 s is far below its applied 4.61 s. The simulation takes that page's LCP as its first paint, where the two coincide in the unthrottled trace.

**What a fix needs**, for the controller and the user to decide:
- fewer bytes ahead of the first paint (the first-load JS, the two display-face preloads on Home);
- or the below-the-fold pictures kept out of the first 400 ms;
- and a measure on the deployed HTTP/2 preview, which Lighthouse can reach only from the user's logged-in browser, since the preview sits behind Vercel's SSO.

## Smoke run (`npm run verify`)

`npm run verify` passed, last after fix round 1 (`024a24a`):
- 1,402 tests;
- the build;
- check:dist, with `/`, the Golden Residence page and both of its block routes enforced, the figures above unchanged (the fix is CSS);
- the smoke run, in 170 s: 23 URLs at 1440×900 and 390×844, the scroll lock, the focus ring, the phone opening (with its text-spacing pass) and the explorer.

**What the smoke run now adds:**
- **The explorer** (`--explorer`, part of a full run). Each step runs in a fresh profile, and a failure names its step:
  - desktop mouse: a real mouse over floor А-5's polygon shows `aside.fxp--a` titled „Блок А · Етаж 5“ within 600 ms; a click opens the dialog „Блок А · Етаж 5“, and Escape closes it;
  - keyboard: Tab from the top reaches a rail, its panel shows its floor, and the rail has a solid 2 px ring;
  - phone, with touch: a tap on floor А-3 shows the sheet titled „Блок А · Етаж 3“ and opens nothing; a second tap opens its dialog;
  - deep link: `/projects/golden-residence/block-a?floor=3` shows the dialog „Блок А · Етаж 3“ after hydration, with no console error or warning.
- **A narrower excuse for dropped requests.** A request Chrome drops (`net::ERR_ABORTED`) passes only if another request of the visit loaded its URL, and only in two cases (Task 6's analysis):
  - Chrome's own background revalidation (initiator `other`);
  - a `<link rel="preload">` of the document that the page fetched for itself.

  The availability store's own fetch (initiator `script`) always fails the run. On `/`, the Golden Residence page and block-a, the only drop seen after a forced garbage collection is Chrome's revalidation of `/api/availability`.
- **No built-in data while the local API answers.** The run first checks that `/api/availability` answers with Golden Residence's four sources `ok`. A page that then shows the stale-data notice fails: its store ended on the data built into the site. Aborting the API and the sheets in a visit to `/` makes that check fail, as it should.
- **The phone opening with text spacing** (`--phone-hero`, part of a full run; added in fix round 1). At each of its four phone screens, after the plain checks, the run turns WCAG 1.4.12's spacing on (below) and fails on:
  - the H1, however many lines it takes, over the Golden Residence info;
  - either half's info across the seam, or the Многофамилна info past the opening's foot;
  - a page wider than the screen (`innerWidth` past the screen's width): anything wider, anywhere on the page, widens the fixed header with it;
  - the header's „За проекта“ or „Меню“ past the screen's edge;
  - an EU logo more than 2 % out of its proportions;
  - the Golden Residence count's digits taller than their one-digit window.

  On the build before the fix it failed 12 times (the overlaps, the Многофамилна info above the seam at all four screens, a 372 and a 373 px page, the header cut at 320). With the fix it passes, and taking any one of the fix's rules out of the built CSS makes it fail again: the headings' wrap (a 372 px page and the header cut), the logos' (squeezed 10 and 11 %), the counts' (60 px digits in a 40 px window).

## Checks by eye

- **No horizontal scroll** on Home or the Golden Residence page at 320, 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440 or 1920 px. `scrollX` stays 0 after a scroll to the right. The phone facade's crop is wider than the screen and clipped.
- **Text spacing (WCAG 1.4.12)**, with line height 1.5, letter spacing 0.12 em, word spacing 0.16 em and paragraph spacing 2 em, all `!important`, holds on Home and the Golden Residence page at every width, and in the header on every page (fix round 1; the first pass found the two failures below at 320–390 px):
  - **The page keeps the screen's width** at 320 and 360 px on all 12 prerendered pages, with the spacing and without: the layout viewport (`innerWidth`) is the screen's width, so the fixed header is too, and „За проекта“ and „Меню“ end 20 px inside its edge.
    - Before, at 320 px the header was 372 px wide and cut both off, on `/`, the Golden Residence page and `/eu-proekt` (349 px), and at 360 px it was 373 px. The header itself fit. A word wider than a phone's line widened the page, and the fixed header with it: the EU poster's „предприятията“ (332 px at 40 px, of 280), the parking's „Паркоместа“ (332 px at 52 px), the services' „ремонтираме,“ (293 px), the story's „апартамента“ (282 px), „500 м“ beside „метростанция“ (320 px), and `/eu-proekt`'s H1.
    - Now a heading's word breaks where it can't fit a phone's line (`overflow-wrap: anywhere` on h1–h3 at 900 px and under, off the legacy pages), and the metro line goes under its figure once the two no longer fit side by side. At the site's own spacing neither changes anything: shots down Home, the Golden Residence page and block-a at 320, 360, 390, 768 and 1440 px are the same with these rules and without them (at most 1/255 on a few pixels).
  - **The header.** At 320 px „За проекта“ takes two lines, and the EU logos keep their proportions. Before, where the page kept its width (`/about` and the other pages), the row narrowed the logos by 10 and 11 % to keep the link on one line. Moved up out of view, the row is clipped to its 36 px, so the link's second line doesn't show its underline along the top of the screen; brought back by the keyboard, it shows whole, with its ring. At 1024–1100 px the header holds as before.
  - **Home's opening on a phone** is a grid whose rows hold their content: the H1, however many lines it takes (three or four with the spacing), then the Golden Residence info, the seam and the Многофамилна info, the opening growing as needed.
    - Before, at 320–390 px the H1 ran over the Golden Residence info, and the Многофамилна info rose above the seam, where its half's clip hid its name.
    - At the site's own spacing every part sits where it did: the same boxes at 390×664, 375×667, 360×640, 320×560, 390×844, 320×760, 768×1024 and 900×700. Shots differ by at most 1/255 on 0.05–0.3 % of the pixels, the overlays' gradients rounding.
    - On a landscape phone (844×390, 740×360), where the opening is its 560 px, the H1 overlapped the Golden Residence info by 10 px even without the spacing. The opening now grows 20 px instead.
  - **The live counts** (RollingNumber, in Home's half, the Golden Residence hero, the explorer and the parking) keep their digits whole. Before, the spacing's line height made each digit's line 1.5 em in its one-em window, which cut the digits' feet off.
  - **The Golden Residence page at 320 px** was 321 px wide even without the spacing: its title's word masks reach .14 em past the words. Chrome showed it zoomed out to 320/321. The hero is clipped across on phones now.
  - The EU row is one line with an ellipsis by design. Between 901 and 1280 px it cuts 92–444 px of the full text even without the overrides, and with them it cuts more, at 768 and 1440 px too. „За проекта“, the way to the full text, stays in view at every width.

## Preview checks on the user's devices

No browser here can stand in for these. The earlier tasks left them for the preview:

- [ ] **Touch laptop** (a fine pointer and a touch screen; Review Focus 3, ruling C9.6): a finger tap on a Home half and on a services column leaves no sticky hover. Neither the widening nor the reveal stays after the tap.
- [ ] **iPhone, Safari** (ruling C9.11): Safari does not focus buttons on tap, so the first tap on a facade rail must preview the floor (the sheet) and only the second open it.
- [ ] **iPhone, Safari**:
  - the doors;
  - `@property` and `:has()` in the opening;
  - the heights of the first screen;
  - the menu, which scrolls on its own.
- [ ] **Android**: the Noto Serif and Roboto fallbacks before the fonts arrive, with no jump when they do.
- [ ] **Firefox and Safari on a desktop**: the doors close and open (the M1 browsers list).

## Follow-ups

- `/api/availability`'s Cache-Control is the CDN's (`s-maxage=30, stale-while-revalidate=300`) with no browser `max-age`, so every load also revalidates it in the background. It needs a contract change, and it is not M2's (ledger).
- The LCP on `/` waits on the controller's decision.
- At 320 px, even without text spacing, the explorer's plan stage and floor sheet are wider than the screen (found in fix round 1, not fixed here). From 360 px both fit.
  - The stage's bar is 367 px wide, its block switch and stepper in one row, so most of its close button and the stepper's ▼ fall past the screen's edge (a deep link to a floor, or to a unit with the panel over the stage).
  - The sheet's action row (the button beside the stepper) needs 299 px of its 284, which widens the sheet's one column, so its close button and the right edge of everything in it are cut by 15 px.
