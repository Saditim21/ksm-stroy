#### Main page `mnogofamilna.html`
1. **The opening `#explorer` is the explorer itself (new composition).**
   - The building is a diptych of its own: two blocks either side of a courtyard. The brass seam runs down that courtyard (x 609.5 of 1200), through the name and the facade.
   - The H1 reads across the seam: „Многофамилна | жилищна *сграда*“.
   - Under it: the address from properties.js and content.txt, the facts pair „144 апартамента в 2 блока“ | „16 свободни по данни към 01.12.2025“, and the actions „Запитване“ (solid) | „По критерии“.
   - Below that, the facade at night, with „Блок А“ and „Блок Б“ over it and a rail of free counts per floor on each outer edge. The rails list floors 2–10 as floor, glyph, count; they are real buttons with spoken names.
   - **Reused from Golden Residence:** the engine and its behaviour.
     - Pointing at a floor dims the rest and tints the floor in its colour; the keyboard gets a dashed lens.
     - The edge panel slides in from that block's edge with the floor's plan in status colours, the counts, the range line or „Разпродаден етаж“, and „Отворете плана“.
     - A click grows the mini plan into the stage.
     - On phones: a Блок А / Блок Б switch with a crop per block. The first tap shows the sheet, the second opens the plan. The sheet closes when the facade leaves the screen.
   - **Why this opening:** it is the most characteristic thing this project has, and it differs from Home (two buildings in two halves) and from Golden Residence (a photo hero, the story, then the explorer).
2. **The lights-off build: kept, as the explorer's resting state.**
   - **What the v2 spec meant (§8.5):** a twin of the dusk render with every window dark. Over it, the original shows only on floors with at least one free apartment. Those floors switch on once, in a short stagger, and the legend reads „Светят етажите със свободни апартаменти“.
   - **Why it serves this page:**
     - It fixes the weakness the concepts README named (nothing showed availability before hover).
     - It is the README's own suggestion ("glowing night facade … as the explorer's resting state").
     - It is honest: Блок А is mostly dark (4 free of 72), Блок Б mostly lit.
     - It belongs to the black-and-gold palette.
   - **How it is built:**
     - I made the twin with sharp from the concept's dusk render, repainting the window pixels as dark glass.
     - The lit render shows through each free floor's band, through an SVG mask.
     - The floors switch on bottom to top after the entrance. This is the page's one signature moment.
     - Under reduced motion the floors are simply lit.
     - Every count and the caption carry „по данни към 01.12.2025“.
3. **`#blokове`: Блок А | Блок Б.** Golden Residence's blocks-in-numbers, sized for this building:
   - 8 squares a floor, floors 10 to 2, a legend with counts, and the types line;
   - production's line „Изберете блок за преглед“ in the lead;
   - „Преглед на блока“ leads to the block page. This replaces production's two block cards.
4. **`#spisak` „Всички 144 апартамента“.** Golden Residence's „По критерии“ list, as a section of its own, because the opening is already the visual way in:
   - filters Блок, Етаж 2–10, Вид, Площ, Изложение and Статус;
   - sorting;
   - the live meta „144 от 144 · 16 свободни“.
5. **`#sgradata`: the building.** A split:
   - left: the street perspective render (photo-2, graded to the night palette);
   - right: H2 „С магазини и подземни *гаражи*“ (the brochure line), production's description, the УПИ address, and the facts 2 / 9 / 144 / 8 with the area range and types, all from the data.
6. **`#galeriya` „Галерия със *снимки*“.** Golden Residence's gallery of two-image spreads that open from the seam:
   - Блок А + Блок Б (photo-3 and photo-4);
   - the front + the perspective (photo-1 and photo-2);
   - the two drawings production shows, whole on umber and never cropped, under production's own titles.

   - „Свържете се с *нас*“ (production's modal title), the lead, phones, email and hours.

#### Block page `mnogofamilna-block-a.html` (new composition: the block as a ledger of floors)
- **Head (a hinge):**
  - left: the back link „Към Многофамилна сграда“ and the H1 „Многофамилна сграда, блок *А*“, as Golden Residence's block route does it;
  - right: „72 апартамента · 9 етажа (2–10) · 8 на етаж“, the count legend and the date.
- **Then a split.** The block's own night facade, with its lights and rail, stays pinned in its own half: А on the left, Б on the right, as the blocks stand. The other half runs the floors from 10 („тавански етаж“) down to 2. Each row holds:
  - a large serif floor number;
  - the count chips;
  - the floor's plan, already in status colours;
  - the range line or „Разпродаден етаж“;
  - „Отворете плана“.
- **Behaviour:**
  - Pointing at a row lights its floor on the facade and draws a brass rule on the row's seam side.
  - On a desktop, scrolling a row to the middle of the screen does the same.
  - A unit, or „Отворете плана“, grows that row's plan into the same stage and unit panel.
- **Why:** production's block page was a picture, a list of floors and hover. This keeps that structure but lets every row show its real plan and status. It also differs from both the main page and Golden Residence's block route.
- **`?block=Б`** renders block Б mirrored (the main page's Б link uses it). `?floor=7` opens a floor and is written bare (spec §7.10).

#### Floors: 9 or 10
I built what the code uses: both blocks with floors 2–10 (`projects.js` `MNOGO_FLOORS`), 8 apartments a floor and 72 a block. Floor 10 is labelled „тавански етаж“, as on the legacy block page. Apartment numbers are the data's own (А-101 is on floor 2).

#### Checks
- **Captures:** 1440, 1280, 1024, 390 and 320 px wide. No console errors, no failed requests, no sideways scroll at any width.
- **Keyboard:** the rails take focus, the arrow keys move between floors, and the dashed lens marks the floor.
- **Reduced motion:** the floors appear lit, with no entrance.
- **Deep links:**
  - `?floor=B-5` opens Б-5;
  - `?floor=Б-11` is ignored;
  - `?unit=a802` opens А-802;
  - an unknown unit is ignored;
  - on the block page, `?floor=7` opens floor 7.
- **Form:** only the invalid path was tested. No valid form was ever submitted.

#### Limits
- The twin was made from the concept's 2400 px render. Some lamp glow and the bright gap of the courtyard remain.
- A lit floor shows the render's own lit windows, not the free apartments one by one.
- The daylight photos have a quick concept grade.

### Questions and defaults

- 9 or 10 floors? Four sources disagree: properties.js:29/:37/:40 say „Блок А - 10 етажа, Блок Б - 9 етажа“; content.txt:7–11 lists А етаж 2–10 and Б етаж 2–9; the block pages and the data give both blocks nine floors with apartments, Б-901…908 included. Default built: the code's floors 2–10 in both blocks (projects.js:48), 72 apartments a block and 144 in all. Floor 10 is labelled „тавански етаж“, as on the legacy block page.
- Floor numbers against apartment numbers: the apartments are numbered by residential level, so А-101 is on етаж 2 and А-901 on етаж 10. Default: the floors read 2–10, as production and content.txt number them, and the apartments keep the sheet's ids. Should buyers see floors 1–9 instead?
- Is Многофамилна still on sale, and what is free today? The only data is the built-in snapshot dated 01.12.2025 (16 of 144 free); the sheets were never published. Default: every count, the lights and the list carry „по данни към 01.12.2025“. Better: the client publishes the А and Б tabs or sends a fresh snapshot. If the building is sold out, the page becomes a completed work (v2 spec §14.2).
- The lights-off render is a concept derivative: I made it with sharp from the concept's 2400 px dusk render. Some lamp glow and the bright gap of the courtyard remain. Default: this file. For the build, the image pipeline should make it from the 3308 px master and someone should check it by eye (v2 spec §11.2).
- Lines the design needed that production lacks, kept plain:
  - the headings „Два блока, по 72 апартамента“ and „Всички 144 апартамента“;
  - the leads „По данни към 01.12.2025. Продадените остават в списъка.“ and „Визуализации на сградата и архитектурните планове.“;
  - the captions „Блок А и блок Б.“, „Двата блока откъм улицата.“ and „Светят етажите със свободни апартаменти, по данни към 01.12.2025.“ (the v2 spec's legend plus the date);
  - the fact labels „апартамента в 2 блока“, „свободни по данни към 01.12.2025“ and the four facts' labels;
  - the hero action „По критерии“ (the spec's tab name) and the back link „Към Многофамилна сграда“;
  - the inquiry lead „Обадете се или изпратете запитване и ще се свържем с вас.“, taken from Golden Residence's lead.
  Default: as built.
- Each apartment's own plan: production has plans for 18 apartments (apartmentImages.js:21–42), but the mapping looks unsure. Б-202 and Б-206 reuse Б-102 and Б-106's plans, file names say floor 8 for some floor-3–7 apartments, and there are entries for А-858 and А-864. Default: the unit panel offers „План на апартамента“ wherever apartmentImages.js maps one. The client should confirm the mapping.
- Garages and parking: the data has 7 places of 15 м² (Гараж А-1…3, Б-1…4; 5 free), but the site hides floor key 0 by the owner's decision, and production shows „Наземни и подземни гаражи“ only as a drawing. Default: no garage counts; the garages drawing is in the gallery. Should they be listed, as on Golden Residence?
- The building PDF: content.txt:3 says „Свали информация и разположение на сградата в PDF:“, but no PDF is in the repo. Default: no download link. Can the client send the PDF?
- Working hours: the legacy block page's modal says „Пон-Пет: 9:00 - 18:00“ (MnogofamilnaSingleBlock.jsx:920), while site.js says „Пн–Пт 8:00–18:00, Сб 9:00–14:00, Нд почивен ден“. Default: site.js's hours, as on every built page.
- Price: production says „По запитване“ (properties.js:38) and the data has no Цена. Default: no price shown; a Цена row appears if the sheet gains one.
- Exposure looks inconsistent: the same position has different exposures on different floors (for example А-101 Север, А-201 Запад/Юг, А-401 Юг/Изток). Default: shown exactly as in the data. The client should check it.

### Design as built

#### Контакти, as four pairs on one seam

The approved idea is that KSM comes in pairs. The contact page has the most literal pairs of all:
- the question and the answer;
- what you ask about and who you are;
- two offices;
- two ways to start.

The brass seam runs unbroken from the header to the footer. Each section reads across it.

Palette, type and motion are fixed by the approved system. The page loads `../2026-11-14-concepts/c2-diptych/css/diptych.css`, `js/core.js`, the fonts and the vendor scripts read-only. It adds only `css/kontakti.css` and `js/kontakti.js`.

### Verdict

The eight new pages read as one site with the approved Diptych Home and Golden Residence. Each opens on its own subject: Продажби on a board of every apartment in both buildings, Многофамилна on its night facade with the floors that have free apartments lit, блок А on its floor rows beside the pinned facade, Контакти on the phones and hours, Обекти on the works archive, За нас on the founding family drawn on the seam, the EU page on the co-funding logos set on the seam, and 404 on a plan with one apartment missing. None copies Home or Golden Residence. They share one header with the EU row, menu and footer, and use the system's heading and text sizes, the brass seam and its motion. The copy is the legacy site's own, with a few plain new lines listed for confirmation. After my fixes the shell is the same on all eight pages (door transitions, menu, footer, EU logos, touch behaviour). No text runs into the page gutter at 1440, 1024, 390, 360 or 320 px, and no page scrolls sideways. Every control shows the 2 px ivory focus ring, and with reduced motion every page is complete. The photos on Обекти and За нас now share one warm grade. Four things need a decision, and most sit outside these files: the read-only Home and Golden Residence concept pages still have the old header, footer and anchor links, the Многофамилна floor count differs between Продажби and the Многофамилна pages, the live open/closed line on Контакти is new copy, and the page titles use more sizes than the system's two. The set is ready for the user to judge by eye.
