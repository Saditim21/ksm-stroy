# M4 results

What M4 (every page still legacy rebuilt in the Diptych system, the legacy removal, and the speed round) built, and how it measures against spec §10 and §12 (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-ksmstroy-m3m4-design.md`). Measured on 2026-11-18 on `redesign-v2`: the "before" figures on Task 7's end (`9efeeaa`), the "after" figures on Task 8's final build (`e9896bd`), and the final review's fix wave (W1–W20) on its own build where it says so. M3 ended at `e6fbeef`; M4's plan commit is `5c0bc06`.

## What was built

- **Task 0, room first.** The browser's chunks are pinned (`scripts/client-chunks.mjs`: `site`, and the explorer's groups `facade` and `explorer`), which freed about 6 KB on every v2 route before M4 added anything; check:dist keeps 512 B of room on every route and caps `/` and Golden Residence at their M3-end figures.
- **Task 1, the shared pieces.** One landing hold for the EU row, one hours source, the closing pair of ways (`Ways`), and the hinge gutter site-wide.
- **Task 2, Контакти** (`/contact`): the question | the answer on the seam, one inquiry form with radio subjects, the construction group and the prefill, its landing and its offices.
- **Task 3, Продажби** (`/projects`): the sales board, every apartment of both buildings as a square, each opening its unit; the buildings compared row by row; the ways.
- **Task 4, Обекти** (`/blog`): the register of the seven works, each opening as a book of its photos (Flip, lazy); the ways.
- **Task 5, За нас** (`/about`): the founding drawn on the seam, the tradition, the team, the figures, the values, the offer, the mission and the work.
- **Task 6, ЕС проект and 404**: the EU page as a document with its budget on the seam; the 404 as the missing room of Golden Residence's typical floor.
- **Task 7, the legacy removal and the leftovers**: framer-motion, the legacy palettes, CSS and plumbing, 34 legacy images and Home's dead slides went (build/client 184 MB → 51 MB); every route is in check:dist's MUST_BE_V2; each page shares its own picture; M2's and M3's leftovers; FW4 measured.
- **Task 8, the speed round** (below), the verification and this note.
- **The final review's fix wave** (W1–W20): the ways' and the rows' one-letter words bound; Продажби's hot floor no longer showing the squares still to rise; Home's stale notice given back its margin; Контакти's construction group never left behind when the page's motion does not run; the Обекти book on short screens, with 44 px thumbnails, a title page that scrolls rather than clips and in the concept's words, and a close during its opening honoured; check:dist's legacy scan over every chunk; the copies' guards; Tailwind's notice and the siblings check; the tracer's panel; and this note's and the captures' README's records.

### Home's steps and the contingencies not taken

Spec §10 works through Home's steps, then the contingencies, on every route that misses. After steps 1, 3 and 5 and the TBT fix no route missed, so these were left, each for its reason:
- **Step 2, Home's below-the-fold pictures out of the window.** Lighthouse already drops their Low-priority requests from its optimistic estimate, `/` is at 2.27 s, and keeping a lazy picture's request back any further would mean leaving it out of the prerendered HTML (the final state) or `content-visibility`, under the reveals' measurements.
- **Step 4, the explorer's CSS out of Home's render-blocking set.** M2's estimate was about −0.03 s, and making a route stylesheet non-blocking would make the prerendered `<link>` differ from the one React renders at hydration.
- **Lighter LCP images, fewer pictures above the fold, font subsetting (`--subset-from`), deferring non-critical CSS:** not needed.
